# Athlete offer placeholders

## Goal

Show how race-scoped partner offers will look before building content management.

## Scope

- Replace the empty Offers placeholder with two clearly labeled sample offers.
- Use the existing Create logo and Storm's official hosted logo.
- Show a discount, code, short description, and external partner link for each offer.
- Keep the layout compact, readable, and responsive from 360px through desktop.
- Do not imply that the sample codes are currently active.

## Validation

- Verify both offers render after entering a race and selecting Offers.
- Verify link destinations and safe external-link attributes.
- Check mobile and desktop layouts, keyboard focus, and console output.
