# Athlete portal home

## Goal

Create a dedicated home page for the athlete portal that follows the established photo-portal pattern: athletes choose a race before entering that race's tools.

## Scope

- Show published races in a compact dropdown with one clear continue action.
- Reuse the photo portal's event image and visual hierarchy.
- Reserve a non-functional top-right member-login position for the future Urban Iron membership experience.
- Preserve the existing race password, heat lookup, check-in, and share-card flows.
- Keep race tabs and editable content management out of this phase.

## Validation

- Verify the empty, loading, selected, and error states.
- Verify keyboard focus and form submission.
- Check 360px, 390px, 430px, and desktop layouts.
- Re-run JavaScript syntax and diff checks before deployment.
