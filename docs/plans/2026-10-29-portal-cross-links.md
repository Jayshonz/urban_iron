# Portal cross-links

## Goal

Give athletes a clear, compact route between the main Urban Iron site, the athlete portal, and the photo platform without opening extra browser tabs.

## Implementation

- Add **Home** and **Photos** to the athlete portal header.
- Keep the Urban Iron logo linked to the athlete portal home.
- Preserve the reserved member-login area and its **Coming soon** status.
- Keep all header targets keyboard accessible and usable at 360, 390, and 430 px.
- Add reciprocal **Home** and **Athletes** links to the photo platform header.

## Verification

- Validate HTML and JavaScript syntax.
- Check keyboard focus and target destinations.
- Check mobile widths at 360, 390, and 430 px plus desktop.
- Verify both production domains after deployment.
